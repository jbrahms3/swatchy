How it works

Three steps, one honest color

No mixing sliders, no eyeballing a color wheel — Swatchy reads the pixel your finger is actually on.

01

Snap or choose a photo

Take a picture, or pick one from your library — whatever has the color you're after.

02

Press to pull the color

Press and drag anywhere on the photo. A loupe zooms in as you go; lift your finger to claim that exact spot.

03

Name it, save it, share it

Swatchy suggests a name from the nearest known color — keep it or rename it, then save it or post it to the feed.

The feed

Every color anyone's claimed, in one place

Discover is a browsable wall of every named color, searchable by name or hex — scroll it like a paint deck someone else already flipped through.

Post a claim to the home feed and people can like it. Press and hold any photo post and a marker appears showing exactly where on the picture the color came from.

The weekly palette

Five colors. One week. Go find them.

Every Monday, Swatchy hands out five colors — the same five for everyone, chosen fresh each week.

Find something in the real world that matches one, photograph it, and press to sample it exactly like claiming a color. Swatchy checks your photo against the target, channel by channel, and scores how far off each of red, green, and blue came out.
